The Analytical Silhouette

Operating entirely as an independent talent, I manage my modeling portfolio exclusively under my own name. Free from traditional agency constraints, this choice mirrors my trajectory across advanced technological spaces—defined by structural autonomy, intellectual precision, and intentional creative choices.

My editorial perspective is deeply shaped by an immersive study of historical fashion frameworks, particularly the structural geometry and gender-defying silhouettes of Yves Saint Laurent. I look at clothing not merely as surface adornment, but as architectural armor that interacts fluidly with space, movement, and light.

"Fashion is an engineering of the body, and modeling is the performance of that geometry."

With complete fluency in Spanish, Italian, and English, I offer luxury fashion houses and creative directors an articulate, globally versatile presence. This linguistic agility ensures collaboration in international sets and heritage design spaces—seamlessly bridging the gap between artistic expression, high-fashion storytelling, and complex technical environments.
